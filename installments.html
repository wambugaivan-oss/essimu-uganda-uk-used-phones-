How Weekly Installments Work

A simple four-step process, done in person at our William Street shop or started on WhatsApp.

STEP 1

Pick your phone

Choose the Samsung, Pixel or iPhone model you want from the pricing tables below.

STEP 2

Pay the first deposit

Pay the listed first deposit in cash or mobile money to secure your device.

Common Questions

What do I need to qualify?

Requirements are set by Android Banja and Zazu Mobile. Message us on WhatsApp with the phone you want and we'll walk you through what each lender needs.

Where do I make my weekly payments?

Weekly payments go through whichever lender approves your plan — Android Banja or Zazu Mobile — using their app or payment channel, not directly to Essimu Uganda.

Can I collect the phone the same day?

Yes, once your first deposit is paid and your lender approves the plan, you can collect your phone from our William Street shop the same day.

Are these phones brand new or UK used?

Both options are available depending on the model — ask us on WhatsApp which condition applies to the phone you want.
